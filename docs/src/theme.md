# Appearance

**Settings → Theme** offers **Field Green**, the new default with paper and forest
surfaces and serif headings; **Slate**, cool blue/teal surfaces and sans-serif
headings; and **Classic Blue**, the familiar navy and blue palette from earlier
releases. All three use the same chart colors and status meanings.
Choose light, dark, or automatic color mode separately. High contrast preserves
its accessibility palette with every theme. Settings search finds “theme”,
“appearance”, or “dark”.

Use **Display mode** beside **Simple / Nerd** at the top of any screen, or open
**Settings → Theme**. Choices stay in sync with setup and apply immediately.

On an existing installation without an explicit theme choice, an upgrade offers
**Keep Classic Blue** or **Use Field Green**. Classic Blue stays active until you choose;
**Decide later** dismisses the prompt for this visit. Your display mode is preserved.
Previously selected themes are kept, and new installations start with Field Green.

| Display mode | Appearance |
|---|---|
| Dark | Dark surfaces and bright text in the selected theme. |
| Light | Light surfaces, dark text, and clear borders. |
| Auto | Follows the operating system's light or dark preference. |
| High contrast | Black and white with reduced visual effects. |

Preferences are stored in this browser, independently of watering settings and
server backups. Another browser can make its own choice. Clearing site data
resets display mode to Dark and may show the theme choice again on an existing
installation. If browser storage is blocked, choices last only for the current visit.

## Read chart colors

The key beside a chart names each measurement and its unit. Blue represents
watering, teal rain, ochre temperature, green soil moisture, and violet pressure
or estimated plant water use (ET), each identified by its own label. Hourly rain
bars show **probability**, not accumulated rain. Irrigation rain bars show amounts;
a vertical mark shows the hold threshold and stripes show measured carryover.
Rain remains **teal before the line**; only the amount beyond it is **amber**.
The marker and **Threshold met** label identify a reached threshold, including
an exact match with no excess. The ET balance uses teal toward the budget and
**green beyond its marker**. These are
individual inputs; the watering verdict still explains the final decision.

In watering decisions, blue means watering, green means confirmed completion, amber means skipped or paused, and gray means off
or unknown. Device health uses green for healthy and red for a fault. The words
and reason carry the meaning alongside the color. Radar and UV retain their own
labeled scales.

Forecasts and soil projections are estimates. Missing data is not zero. In History,
focus a line chart and use Left/Right, Home/End, or a pointer to inspect its values.
